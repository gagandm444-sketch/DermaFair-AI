Mathematical FormulationSelvaraju et al.

Grad-CAM calculates the gradient of the class score y^c with respect to feature maps A^k in the final convolutional layer.

1. Importance Weight (α_k^c)

α_k^c = (1 / Z) Σ_i Σ_j (∂y^c / ∂A_ij^k)

Global Average Pooling of gradients across spatial dimensions i, j
2. ReLU Activation Heatmap

L_GradCAM^c = ReLU( Σ_k α_k^c · A^k )

Rectified Linear Unit isolates features positively contributing to class c
Eliminating Shortcut Learning
Why Explainability Protects Patients
  • Surgical Skin Pen Markings: Deep models often memorize blue surgical ink as a predictor for cancer. Grad-CAM confirms attention is on the lesion, not pen ink.
  • Dermoscopy Rulers: Measurement markings frequently correlate with biopsy decisions in historical datasets. Grad-CAM prevents ruler-based predictions.
  • Hair Artifacts: Dark hair follicles can trigger false-positive melanoma alerts without morphological verification.
Key Insight: Grad-CAM explains where the model looked; clinical validation requires board-certified dermatologist review.

About the DermaFair AI Project

Explainable Multi-Class Skin Disease Classification with Algorithmic Fairness Analysis Across Diverse Skin Tones.

The Core Problem

Skin diseases affect over 1.9 billion people globally. Most existing AI classifiers fail clinically:

  • Black Box Blindness: Doctors cannot trust AI recommendations without visual evidence of why the model predicted a diagnosis.
  • Skin-Tone Bias: Dark skin tones (Fitzpatrick V–VI) suffer elevated false-negative rates because historical datasets over-represent fair skin.
  • Binary Oversimplification: Grouping thousands of conditions into simple 'benign vs malignant' fails everyday clinical triage.
The DermaFair Solution

Our framework incorporates four technological pillars:

  • 23-Class Categorization: Maps dermatoses into 23 distinct clinical families.
  • Grad-CAM Verification: Verifies convolutional attention boundaries to eliminate artifact learning.
  • Fitzpatrick Fairness Audit: Continuous disparity telemetry across skin tones I–VI.
  • DermaBot AI Assistant: Provides automated clinical healing guides and personalized daily routines for clear skin.
